Once an inquiry is sent, SourceReady reads each supplier's reply and pulls their answers into a structured quote. Open any inquiry from the inquiry list to see its progress.

An inquiry has three tabs:

<Columns cols={3}>
  <Card title="Overview" icon="gauge">
    Progress at a glance.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Invitations" icon="envelope">
    Each supplier's status and conversation.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Compare quotes" icon="scale-balanced">
    Every quote side by side.
  </Card>
</Columns>

Click **Add supplier** at the top right to invite more suppliers at any time.

## Overview

<Frame caption="The overview shows products, invited suppliers, and how far each one has got.">
  ![Inquiry overview for Raisin Container with selected product, invited suppliers, received quotes, top quote score, and invitation counts](/images/inquiry-overview.png)
</Frame>

- **Selected product**: the products being quoted, with target price and quantity.
- **Invited suppliers**: each supplier's status and how many questions they've answered, for example _0/5_.
- **Received quotes** and **Top quote score**: how many quotes are in, and the best score so far.
- **Pending invite**, **Invitation sent**, **Invitation viewed**, and **Connected**: how many suppliers are at each stage.
- **Basic info**, **Preset questions**, and **Advance settings**: edit the inquiry's details, questions, and [follow-up rules](/follow-up-rules).

Click the arrow on a card to open its details.

## Invitations

The **Invitations** tab lists every invited supplier and where they are in the conversation.

<Frame caption="Each supplier's quotation status, follow-ups, and last contact.">
  ![Invitations tab with Southco India marked Connected and three suppliers marked Viewed](/images/invitations.png)
</Frame>

### Quotation status

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | Not sent yet |
| **Sent** | Email delivered |
| **Viewed** | The supplier opened the email |
| **Auto response** | The supplier's mailbox sent an automatic reply, such as an out-of-office message |
| **Connected** | The supplier replied |
| **Request failed** | The email couldn't be sent. The **Last message status** column shows why, for example **Email not connected** or **Authorization expired** |

Use the segments (**All**, **Sent**, **Viewed**, **Connected**, and **More**) to filter by status.

Other columns show **Follow ups** (how many AI reminders have gone out), **Last contact**, answered questions, and the AI's **Recommend value**. Click **Fields** to show more, such as contact info or the supplier's certifications and export markets.

### Talk to a supplier

Click **Chat** on a supplier's row to open the email conversation. You can read the whole thread and reply yourself, in addition to anything the AI sends.

## Review a quote

Click a supplier to open **Quotation details**.

<Frame caption="Quotation details: score, product, tasks, and each answer.">
  ![Quotation details for Southco India showing recommend value, inquiry product, tasks, and answered questions](/images/quotation-details.png)
</Frame>

- **Recommend value**: the AI's score for this quote, out of 100. Click it to see the reasoning.
- **Inquiry product**: the product, quantity, and target unit price being quoted.
- **Tasks**: when the AI needs something from you to keep the conversation moving, such as a decision or a missing detail, it creates a task here.
- **Questions**: each preset question with the supplier's answer. Filter by **Pending**, **Answered**, or **Unanswered**, and click **View details** to expand an answer.

<Note>
  Each time a supplier replies and AI updates their quote answers, 5 credits are used.
</Note>

## Compare quotes

The **Compare quotes** tab puts every quote in one table: one row per supplier, one column per question. Rows are sorted by **Recommend value** by default, so the strongest quotes are on top.

<Frame caption="Compare unit price, lead time, and every other answer across suppliers.">
  ![Compare quotes table with recommend value and columns for unit price and production lead time](/images/compare-quotes.png)
</Frame>

### Approve or disapprove

Use the icons at the end of each row:

- **✓ Approve** a quote you want to move forward with.
- **✕ Disapprove** a quote you're ruling out.
- Changed your mind? Use **Undo approval** or **Undo disapproval**.

Filter the table with **All**, **Approved**, and **Disapproved**.

### Export

Click **Export** to download all quotes as an Excel file, including the AI assessment for each supplier. Share it with your team or keep it with your order records.

<Note>
  Exporting quotes is available on the Pro Plus plan.
</Note>

## Ask the AI about your quotes

Use the **Ask anything** bar at the bottom of the page to ask about the inquiry in plain language:

```text
Which supplier offers the best price for a 30-day lead time?
```

```text
Summarize the differences between the top three quotes.
```

See [Global chat](/global-chat) for more on what the in-page assistant can do.
