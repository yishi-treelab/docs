SourceReady includes customs and bill-of-lading records: individual shipments between exporters and importers around the world. Ask about them in plain language to check a supplier's real export history, find who supplies a brand, or size a trade flow.

Each shipment record can include:

- Shipment date and bill-of-lading IDs
- Exporter and importer names and countries
- Product description, HS code, and category
- Origin and destination
- Weight, quantity, and declared value (CIF, in USD)
- Transport, vessel, and container details

## Combine with supplier search

Trade data works best alongside [supplier search](/supplier-search):

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find candidates">
    ```text
    Chinese sock manufacturers exporting to Germany or the Netherlands
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify with shipments">
    ```text
    For the top 5, show their actual shipments to Germany in the last 12 months.
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare">
    ```text
    Compare them by shipment count, main buyers, and average shipment weight.
    ```
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  In the manual filter, turn on **Shipments → Customs data available** to only see suppliers with trade records. You can also open any supplier profile and check its **Shipments** tab.
</Tip>

## Data coverage

<Warning>
  Trade data is powerful but not complete. Keep these limits in mind:

  - **Records start on January 1, 2021.** Questions about earlier years are out of range.
  - **Recent months can lag by 1–4 months**, depending on the country.
  - **Country coverage varies.** Some countries publish partial data, and some trade flows are inferred from the partner country's records.
  - **Fields can be sparse or inconsistent.** Descriptions, categories, and weights come from customs declarations, so read them with care.
  - **Not every exporter is linked to a supplier profile yet.** The agent can still search by company name.
</Warning>

If you ask about a time range the data doesn't cover, the agent tells you instead of guessing.
