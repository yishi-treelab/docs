Getting a full quote often takes several emails. SourceReady's AI can handle that back-and-forth for you:

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="AI follow up" icon="clock-rotate-left">
    Sends a reminder when a supplier hasn't replied for a while.
  </Card>

  <Card title="AI auto reply" icon="reply">
    Answers supplier questions and asks for missing details when a supplier does reply.
  </Card>
</Columns>

Both use your [knowledge base](/knowledge-base) and the inquiry's details, so their replies are accurate and match your brand.

## Where to set them

Each inquiry has its own rules. Set them in **Advance settings**:

- **While sending**: in the **Send inquiry** window, use the **AI follow up** and **AI auto reply** toggles, or click **Rules & bases** for the full settings.
- **After sending**: on the inquiry's **Overview**, find the **Advance settings** card.

## AI follow up

AI follow up is **on by default** for new inquiries.

### When to send

**Send the message when the supplier hasn't replied for over** a set time, in **Hour(s)** or **Day(s)**. The default is 24 hours.

### When to stop

**Keep sending it until** any of the checked conditions is met:

| Condition | Default |
| --- | --- |
| **Reaching** _N_ **day(s) after** the **Order due date** or **Created date**. Use 0 for the same day | Off |
| **They've received the message for** _N_ **times** | On, 3 times |
| **Required questions are answered**, **All questions are answered**, or **The supplier is connected** (their status is **Connected**) | On, required questions |

<Tip>
  Mark your must-have questions as **Required** in [Preset questions](/create-and-send-an-inquiry#preset-questions). The AI then stops chasing a supplier as soon as it has the answers you actually need.
</Tip>

## AI auto reply

AI auto reply is **off by default**. When it's on, the AI answers supplier replies for you, such as questions about your specs, quantities, or shipping. It also asks for any answers that are still missing.

**Keep replying to suppliers until**:

- **All questions are answered** (default), or
- **Required questions are answered**

**Keep replying to suppliers**:

- **Any time** (default), or
- **Until reaching** _N_ **day(s) after** the **Order due date** or **Created date**

When auto reply is active, the inquiry shows **AI is auto replying**.

## Custom prompt

Both AI follow up and AI auto reply have an optional **Custom prompt**. Use it to control how the AI writes, for example:

```text
Write every email in both English and Chinese. Keep follow-ups under 80 words and always mention our order due date.
```

## Auto cc

Add email addresses under **Auto cc** to copy them on every email sent to suppliers in this inquiry. This is useful for keeping a manager or a shared sourcing inbox in the loop.
