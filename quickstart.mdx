In this guide, you'll ask the AI agent to find suppliers, check one supplier's profile, and save the ones you like to a list. It takes about five minutes.

<Note>
  Agent searches use credits. The Free plan includes 200 credits plus 30 more every day, which covers several searches. See [pricing](https://www.sourceready.com/pricing) for credit costs.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    [Sign up for SourceReady](https://app.sourceready.com/api/auth/login?screen_hint=signup) with your work email. A workspace is created for you automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a supplier search">
    Click **New chat** in the sidebar, then choose **Supplier search** in the chat box.

    Describe the supplier you need. The more specific you are, the better the matches. Include:

    - **Product:** what you want made, including materials or key specs
    - **Location:** countries or regions you want to source from
    - **Requirements:** minimum order quantity (MOQ), certifications, or production capacity

    For example:

    > Find manufacturers in Vietnam that make seamless women's leggings, accept orders under 500 units, and have OEKO-TEX certification.

    <Tip>
      You don't have to pick a skill. If you just type a request, the agent chooses the right one. Picking **Supplier search** makes the agent focus on finding manufacturers.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review a supplier's profile">
    The agent returns a list of matching suppliers. Click a supplier to open their profile. Depending on the data available, you'll see these tabs:

    - **Overview:** what the company makes, where it's based, and how well it matches your request
    - **Products:** items the supplier makes or sells
    - **Customers:** brands the supplier ships to, based on trade data
    - **Certifications:** quality and compliance certificates
    - **Shipments:** customs records of past shipments
    - **Insights:** trends from the supplier's trade history
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save suppliers to a list">
    Click **Save to list** on the profile, or on a row in the results, and pick a list or create a new one.

    Saved suppliers appear under **Supplier** in the sidebar, where you can filter them and add notes for your team.
  </Step>
</Steps>
