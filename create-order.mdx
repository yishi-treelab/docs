You'll fill in the **New order** form and submit it. Use this for a one-off order, or when the PO isn't in a file you can upload. For several orders at once, [upload your POs](/upload-po) instead.

## Fill in the form

Go to **Order → Orders** and click **Add order**. The form has up to five sections.

    Add one line per style:

    - **Add empty line** lets you type the **Style #**, **Quantity**, price, and other line fields yourself.
  </Step>

## Save as draft or submit

| Button | What happens | What it needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Save as draft** | Saves the order as **Draft** and keeps the form open, so you can finish it later | A PO # or Order name |
| **Submit** | Creates the order as **In progress**, with its milestones, and opens its [detail page](/order-details) | A PO # or Order name, a supplier, and a **Style #** and **Quantity** on every line |

If something is missing, the fields are marked **Required** and you'll see "Missing required fields" or "Select a supplier to submit".

<Note>
  Milestones are added when you submit. A draft saved without a profile has no milestones yet.
</Note>

## Edit an order

Open the order and click the pencil icon (**Edit order**) in the header. The same form opens with the order's details.

- For a submitted order, change what you need and click **Save**.
- For a draft, you'll still see **Save as draft** and **Submit**.

To update one milestone, you don't need the form. Click the step on the order's [milestone stepper](/order-details#update-a-milestone) instead.
