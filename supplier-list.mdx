The supplier list is your team's shared supplier database. It holds suppliers you saved from SourceReady's global database and private suppliers you added yourself. Open it from **Suppliers** in the sidebar.

## Organize suppliers into lists

Lists group suppliers by project, product, or anything else your team tracks. They appear as tabs across the top of the page.

- **All suppliers** shows every supplier in your workspace.
- Each saved list, such as _Ikea Wooden Stool Suppliers_, has its own tab. Click **All lists** to see every list.
- Click **\+** to create a new list.
- Use the **⋯** menu on a list tab to rename or delete it.

A supplier can belong to more than one list. Removing a supplier from a list doesn't delete it from your workspace.

## Bulk actions

Select one or more suppliers with the checkboxes, and an action bar appears at the bottom of the page.

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Ask AI** | Opens the AI agent with the selected suppliers as context, for example to compare them |
| **Send inquiry** | Starts a new [inquiry](/create-and-send-an-inquiry) to the selected suppliers. Use the arrow to add them to an existing inquiry instead |
| **Save to list** | Adds the selected suppliers to a list |
| **Export** | Downloads the selected suppliers as an Excel file |
| **⋯** | More actions, such as **Remove from list** |

<Note>
  **Export** is available on the Pro Plus plan. You can export up to 500 suppliers at a time. Exporting locked suppliers unlocks their contact details and advanced fields, which uses credits. The export dialog shows the expected credit cost before you confirm.
</Note>

## Supplier profile

Click a supplier to open its profile. The tabs shown depend on the data available for that supplier.

<Frame caption="A supplier profile with highlights, customers, and contact details.">
  ![Supplier Detail](/images/supplier-detail.png "Supplier Detail")
</Frame>

| Tab | What you'll find |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Company description, highlights, and key facts |
| **Customers** | Brands and buyers the supplier ships to, with shipment counts |
| **Certifications** | Certifications such as BSCI, FSC, or ISO 9001 |
| **Shipments** | Customs records: shipment count, export volume, export countries, and individual bills of lading |
| **Insights** | Charts built from trade data, such as export volume over time, export countries, CIF value, and customer cooperation |
| **Extension fields** | Your workspace's [custom fields](/supplier-custom-fields) for this supplier |

A small badge next to a fact, such as **✧ 1**, shows how many web sources back it. Click it to see the evidence.

<Frame caption="The Shipments section shows trade data from customs records.">
  ![Supplier Shipments](/images/supplier-shipments.png "Supplier Shipments")
</Frame>

### Contact details

The **Contact** card on the right lists emails, phone numbers, and websites.

- **Public** contacts come from SourceReady's database.
- **Private** contacts are ones your team added.

Viewing a supplier's public contact details costs 20 credits the first time. After that, they stay unlocked for your workspace. Starting an inquiry with a locked supplier unlocks its contacts the same way, at the same cost.
