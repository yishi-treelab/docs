Use **Product ideation** to create and edit product images, and **Product video** to turn an image into a short video. Both work from a text description, a reference image, or both. You can use them for early concepts, supplier briefs, or marketing drafts.

## Product ideation (images)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Product ideation">
    Click **Product ideation** on the input bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the concept">
    Include materials, colors, style, packaging, and the setting you want.

    ```text
    Design a luxury scented candle collection with minimalist packaging, natural soy wax, earthy tones, premium lifestyle branding
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Iterate">
    Ask for changes in plain language, such as a new colorway, material, logo placement, or background, and the agent generates new versions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

</Frame>

### Edit an existing image

Upload a product photo or a previously generated image and describe the change:

```text
Change the jacket to a matte black finish and add a reflective logo on the chest
```

```text
Place this can on a picnic table in bright summer light
```

## Product video

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Product video">
    Click **Skills → Product video**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an image and describe the motion">
    Upload a product image, or use one you generated earlier in the chat, then describe the camera movement, lighting, and mood.

    ```text
    Create a video with the jacket rotating under soft lighting to highlight the gradient colors and texture
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview and download">
    The video appears in the chat when it's ready. Click to play it, or ask for another version with different motion or lighting.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="An 8-second vertical (9:16) product video generated from a single product image.">
  ![Video Generation](/images/video-generation.png "Video Generation")
</Frame>

<Tip>
  Mention the aspect ratio you need: **9:16** for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts, or **16:9** for web and YouTube.
</Tip>

## From concept to supplier

Generated images carry straight into sourcing. In the same chat, try:

```text
Find suppliers who can make this design with MOQ under 500
```

```text
Find existing products that look like this concept
```

<Note>
  Generated images and videos are concepts, not photos of real products. Confirm materials, colors, and feasibility with suppliers before you commit to production.
</Note>
