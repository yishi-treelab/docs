A **WIP sheet** is the spreadsheet a supplier fills in with progress on your orders: actual dates, revised dates, and notes for each milestone. When a filled-in sheet comes back, SourceReady reads it and proposes changes to the matching orders.

## Three ways a filled-in sheet gets in

| How it arrives | What happens | When to use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **You upload it** (this page) | AI reads it. You review the changes and click **Apply** | The supplier sent the sheet some other way, such as WeChat or a shared drive |
| **The supplier replies in a WIP tracker thread** | Milestones are updated automatically, with no review step. See [WIP tracker](/wip-tracker#when-the-supplier-replies) | You sent the sheet from a WIP tracker |

## Upload and extract

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Import WIP">
    Click **Upload WIP sheet** at the top of the [Orders list](/orders) or the WIP tracker list. You can also click **Import WIP (Excel)** on the **Imports** page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the spreadsheet">
    Click **Upload spreadsheet** and choose the file. You can upload Excel (`.xlsx`, `.xls`) or `.csv` files. It shows **Ready to extract**.

    <Frame caption="A supplier WIP sheet ready to extract.">
      ![Import WIP page with a Spreadsheet section listing Northshore WIP 2026-10-01.xlsx as Ready to extract](/images/wip/upload-wip-setup.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add AI instructions (optional)">
    Under **Instructions**, type any hints for reading this file in **AI instructions**, such as "Only read the season sheets; the order line key is Style + Color." They're added after your saved [import instructions](/import-instructions) and win where they disagree.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Extract">
    AI reads the sheet, matches each row to an order by **PO #** and **Style #**, and works out what changed. The review page opens when it's done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The sheet doesn't have to be in SourceReady's format. AI can read a supplier's own layout, as long as each row has a PO number or style number it can match.

## Review the changes

The review page shows the file, its status (**Needs Review**), and a summary such as "8 changes across 3 orders". Below that, one row per matched order.

<Frame caption="Proposed changes from a WIP sheet, one row per order.">
  ![Review page for Northshore WIP 2026-10-01.xlsx with status Needs Review, 8 changes across 3 orders, and a table of orders with health, number of changes, and from-to values for Comments, TOP SAMPLE SENT, and START CUT](/images/wip/upload-wip-review.png)
</Frame>

Each row shows:

- **PO #** and **Style**.
- **Health**: the order's health now, and what it will be after the changes, such as **On track → At risk**.
- **Changes**: how many changes are proposed.
- One column per changed field or milestone, with the old and new value, such as **Sep 27 → Sep 29**.

### Skip or edit a change

<Frame caption="An order expanded, with all of its changes skipped.">
  ![Review table with one order unchecked showing 0 of 3 changes, expanded to list each change crossed out with Edit and Restore buttons and an Open order link](/images/wip/upload-wip-skipped.png)
</Frame>

- **Uncheck an order** to skip every change on it.
- **Click the arrow** next to an order to see each change on its own line. Each line shows where the value came from (the sheet's file name), and a quote of the source text when there is one.
- Click **Skip** to leave out one change, or **Restore** to bring it back.
- Click **Edit** to correct a value before applying it. Edited values are marked **edited**.
- Click **Open order ↗** to go to the order's detail page.

To see exactly what AI read, click **Show rows read from the file** at the bottom.

### Orders that didn't match

If a row's PO # or style isn't in SourceReady, it's listed under **N orders not in SourceReady**. Each one shows its PO and style, which rows it came from, and its proposed changes. If it's an order you do have, under a slightly different number, click **Link to order**, search for the right order, and pick it. Its changes then join the review.

## Apply or dismiss

<Frame caption="The apply bar, with three changes skipped.">
  ![Footer with Cancel, Dismiss, a 3 skipped count, and an Apply 2 orders button](/images/wip/upload-wip-apply-bar.png)
</Frame>

The bar at the bottom counts what you skipped and edited.

- Click **Apply** (such as **Apply 2 orders**) to write the changes. Each order's milestones, health, and risk update straight away, and its [Activity](/order-details#activity) shows a milestone update from the upload.
- Click **Dismiss** to throw the import away without changing anything.
- Click **Cancel** to leave and come back later. The import stays in [import history](/upload-po#import-history) as **Processing**.

## Read the sheet again

If AI misread the sheet, click **···** at the top right of the review and choose **Reprocess**. Each run of the same file is kept. When there's more than one, a run switcher appears next to the file name, so you can compare runs. The newest is marked latest.

If the same fix applies to most of your suppliers' sheets, save it once in [import instructions](/import-instructions) instead.
