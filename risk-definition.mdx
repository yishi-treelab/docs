A **risk tag** is a rule that flags a problem on an order, such as a late milestone or a quality issue in a supplier note. Each tag has a severity, and the tags that match an order decide its **health**: the At risk, Watch, or On track label you see on the Orders list.

You'll set up risk tags under **Settings → Order → Risk definition**.

<Frame caption="The Risk definition tab, with the built-in overdue tag selected.">
  ![Risk definition settings with the Overdue tag selected: label, description, tag key, High severity, Enabled switch, and a rule that matches when any step is overdue by more than 1 day](/images/wip/risk-rule-based.png)
</Frame>

## The tag every workspace starts with

New workspaces come with one high-severity tag that matches when **any milestone is more than 1 day past its plan date and not completed**. It's an ordinary tag: you can change its threshold, lower its severity, turn it off, or delete it.

## How tags become health

Each order's health comes from the most severe tag that matches it.

Health shows in the **Health** column on the [Orders list](/orders), feeds the **Needs attention** count, and is explained in the **Risk** section of each [order's detail page](/order-details#risk).

## Add a tag

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Add tag">
    A new tag opens in the editor with a sample rule.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Fill in **Label** (what people see on the order, such as "FRI at risk") and **Description** (why it matters). The description is shown on the order when the tag matches, so write it for the person reading the order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the tag key and severity">
    **Tag key** is a short id, such as `fri_at_risk`. Pick **High**, **Medium**, or **Low** in **Severity**, using the health table above.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the rule">
    Choose **Rule-based** or **LLM** under **Rule**. Both are explained below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Create tag** (or **Save tag** when you edit one). Leave **Enabled** on for the tag to be checked.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Rule-based tags

A rule-based tag matches when its conditions are true. Each condition reads **Where** _field_ _operator_ _value_. Click **\+ Add condition** to add another, and choose **And** or **Or** to join it to the one above.

The fields you can check:

| Field | What it checks |
| --- | --- |
| **Any step overdue (days, not completed)** | How many days late the worst open milestone is |
| **Any step delay (days)** | How many days late any milestone is, including completed ones |
| **Any step state** | A milestone's state, such as done or late |
| **Any step approval status** | The approval wording on any milestone |
| **Any step notes** | The note text on any milestone |
| **Last step state** | The state of the final milestone |
| **Order status** | Draft, In progress, Completed, or Cancelled |
| Order dates | **Ex-factory date**, **Revised ex-factory date**, **Customer ship date**, **Revised customer ship date**, **Order date**, **Material in-house date**, **ETD**, **ETA port** |
| **Order total** | The order's total value |
| **Now** | Today's date, for comparing against an order date |
| **Custom field (enter path)** | One of your [custom order fields](/order-fields#add-a-custom-field). Type its key, such as `cus_fabric_mill` |

Dates and numbers use **Equals**, **Not equals**, **Greater than**, **Greater or equal**, **Less than**, **Less or equal**, **Is empty**, and **Is not empty**. Text fields use **Equals**, **Not equals**, **Contains**, **Is empty**, and **Is not empty**.

To compare two fields, change **Value** to **Another field**. For example, "Where **Revised ex-factory date** **Greater than** **Another field** **Ex-factory date**" flags orders whose ship date has slipped.

<Tip>
  To make a softer warning before the overdue tag fires, add a **Medium** tag with **Any step overdue (days, not completed)** **Greater than** `0`. Late steps then show as Watch first, and At risk once they pass your High threshold.
</Tip>

## LLM tags

An LLM tag uses a prompt instead of conditions. Use it for things a rule can't see, such as a supplier note that mentions a failed lab dip.

<Frame caption="An LLM tag that flags quality or material problems mentioned in supplier notes.">
  ![Risk tag editor in LLM mode with the label Quality or material issue, Medium severity, and a prompt describing when the tag should match](/images/wip/risk-llm.png)
</Frame>

Choose **LLM** under **Rule**, then describe in **Prompt** when the tag should match. The model reads the order's fields and current milestones and answers yes or no. Be specific about what counts, for example: "Match when any supplier note mentions a failed shade or lab dip, fabric re-dye, a rejected sample, or failed testing."

## When tags are checked

SourceReady checks an order's tags when:

- The order is created, or added by an [upload](/upload-po).
- You edit the order or one of its milestones.
- A supplier update is applied from a [WIP sheet](/upload-wip-sheet), the [Inbox](/receive-via-inbox), or a [WIP tracker](/wip-tracker) reply.
- You click **Re-run risk check** in the **Risk** section of the order, or in its **More** menu.

Changes to a tag apply the next time an order is checked. To see a new or edited tag on an order straight away, open the order and click **Re-run risk check**.

<Frame caption="The Risk section on an order, showing which tag matched and which milestones caused it.">
  ![Risk section of an order showing At risk 22 days late, one risk flagged, the Overdue tag with High severity, and chips for the late milestones](/images/wip/order-detail-risk.png)
</Frame>

## Turn off or delete a tag

Turn off **Enabled** to stop checking a tag but keep it for later. Click **Delete** to remove it. Either way, it stops matching the next time each order is checked.
