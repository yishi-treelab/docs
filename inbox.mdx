The **Inbox** watches a Gmail or Outlook mailbox you connect. Each incoming email is sorted into a category. Order updates are read automatically and turned into proposed changes, which wait for your review. Nothing changes on an order until someone clicks **Apply**.

Open it from **Order → Inbox** in the left sidebar.

## Connect a mailbox

You'll do this once, under **Settings → Order → Inbox**. You can also click the gear icon on the Inbox page.

<Frame caption="Inbox settings with an Outlook mailbox connected.">
  ![Inbox settings with Enable inbox turned on, a Mailbox selected, Folder set to All incoming, Test connection and Manage connectors, and a Categories section with the built-in Order update category](/images/wip/inbox-settings.png)
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect a Gmail or Outlook account">
    If you haven't connected one yet, click **Manage connectors** and add the account. Use a mailbox your suppliers send updates to, such as a shared `wip-updates@` address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on the inbox">
    Turn on **Enable inbox**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the mailbox and folder">
    Choose the account in **Mailbox**. In **Folder**, leave **All incoming**, or pick one folder if you route supplier mail there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it">
    Click **Test connection**. You'll see **Connected** with the address if it works.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Until a mailbox is connected, the Inbox page shows **Set up your inbox** with a link to these settings.

### Help AI read your suppliers' emails

Under **Order update**, type hints in **Notes about these emails (optional)**. For example: "Acme sends WIP updates with 'WIP' in the subject and quotes only the last 5 digits of the PO. 'Ship date' means ex-factory."

### Add your own category

Click **Add category**, then type a **Name** (such as "QC report") and a **Description** of which emails belong in it (such as "Inspection results or QC photos from a factory or inspection agency."). You can add up to 20. Click **Save categories** when you're done.

Use the switch on a category to turn it off, or click **Remove** to delete one you added.

## The Inbox page

The left side lists emails. Click **All** to see everything, or **Needs Review** to see only items waiting for you. Click the refresh icon to check for new mail.

Each item shows its subject, sender, category, when it arrived, and a summary such as "19 changes across 3 orders". The icon next to the summary shows where the changes came from: an **Excel attachment** or the **Email body**.

Click an item to review it on the right. See [Receive via inbox](/receive-via-inbox) for how to review and apply it.

**Needs Review** shows items that are **Needs Review** or **Failed**.

### More actions

Click **···** at the top right of an item:

- **Reprocess** reads the email again, for example after you add notes to the Order update category.
- **View import rows** opens the rows AI read from an attached WIP sheet.
