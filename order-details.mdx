Click any order on the [Orders list](/orders) to open its detail page. Here you'll see why the order is at risk, update its milestones, and review its history.

## The header

<Frame caption="The order header and tabs.">
  ![Order header with the PO number, supplier, product, and owner, the Email supplier, Activity, Edit, and More buttons, and the Milestones, Basic info, Files, and Updates tabs](/images/wip/order-detail-header.png)
</Frame>

The header shows the PO #, its status (such as **In progress**), any risk tags that matched (such as **Overdue**), and the supplier, product, and owner.

## Milestones tab

### Risk

The **Risk** section explains the order's health. It shows the health label, how many risks were flagged, and when they were last checked.

<Frame caption="One risk tag matched, caused by five late milestones.">
  ![Risk section showing At risk 22 days late, 1 risk flagged, last evaluated time, and the Overdue tag with High severity listing late milestones such as PP SAMPLE SENT +22d](/images/wip/order-detail-risk.png)
</Frame>

Each matched tag shows its severity, why it matched, and the milestones that caused it, worst first. Click a milestone chip to jump to that step. Click **Show details** or **Hide details** to expand or collapse the list.

Click **Re-run risk check** to check the order again now, for example after you change a [risk tag](/risk-definition).

### Update a milestone

Hover over a step and click the pencil icon to edit it.

<Frame caption="Editing a milestone.">
  ![Edit dialog for PP SAMPLE SENT with Plan date, Actual date, Notes, and Status set to Auto (from dates), with the hint Currently Delayed, worked out from the dates](/images/wip/order-detail-step-edit.png)
</Frame>

The fields you see depend on the switches set for that step in its [milestone profile](/milestones#create-a-profile):

      <td>When it actually happened</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>**Approval status**</td>
      <td>Such as "approved" or "pending"</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>**Notes**</td>
      <td>A remark, such as the supplier's explanation for a delay</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>**Status**</td>
      <td>Leave it on **Auto (from dates)** to let the dates decide, or override it</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>

Click **Save**. The order's health and risk are checked again straight away.

<Note>
  How **Status** works:

  - On **Auto (from dates)**, a step with an actual date is **Completed**, and a step past its plan date without one is **Delayed**.
  - Setting an **Actual date** always makes the step **Completed**, whatever you choose in Status.
  - **In progress** or **Pending** past the plan date still shows as **Delayed**.
  - Choose **Skipped / not required** for a step that doesn't apply to this order. Skipped steps don't count as late.
</Note>

### Step history

Click a node to see its history: who changed it, when, and what changed. For example, "Excel import · state pending → completed · actual 2026-08-18". Click **Close** to hide it.

## Basic info tab

Everything else about the order, in three sections:

- **Order header**: every order field, including your [custom fields](/order-fields).
- **Supplier & factory**: the linked supplier, its contact, the owner, and the customer. Click **View supplier** to open the supplier's profile.
- **Line items**: each style with its item name, material, quantity, and price.

<Frame caption="The Basic info tab.">
  ![Basic info tab with the Order header field grid, the Supplier and factory card, and a Line items table](/images/wip/order-detail-basic-info.png)
</Frame>

## Files tab

**Source files** lists the PO or WIP sheet files this order was created or updated from. Click the download icon to get a copy. **Attachments** lists files added to the order.

## Updates tab

Shows the [WIP tracker](/wip-tracker) this order belongs to. Click **Open tracker** to go to it. If the order isn't in an active tracker, click **Create WIP tracker** to start one with this order. Earlier trackers are listed under **Past trackers**.

## Emails

Click **Email supplier** to see the email thread of this order's WIP tracker, where you can write to the supplier and read their replies. If the order isn't in a tracker yet, you'll be asked to add it to one first.

## Activity

Click the clock icon to open **Activity**. It lists everything that's happened to the order, newest first:

- **Order created** and **Order updated**, with who did it and which fields changed.
- **Milestone update**, with where it came from (such as an upload, a tracker email, or the Inbox), the file name, and each change.
- **Risk analysed**, with the tags that matched and the late steps.

<Frame caption="The Activity sidebar.">
  ![Activity sidebar listing Risk analysed, Milestone update from an uploaded WIP file with each step change, Order updated, and Order created](/images/wip/order-detail-activity.png)
</Frame>
