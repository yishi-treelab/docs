Each inquiry bundles the products you want quoted, the suppliers you're asking, and the questions they need to answer. SourceReady sends a personalized email to each supplier from your connected mailbox.

<Info>
  Connect your Gmail or Outlook account first. See [Set up guide](/inquiry-set-up-guide#1-connect-your-email).
</Info>

## Start an inquiry

You can start from wherever you're working:

| From | How |
| --- | --- |
| **Inquiry list** | Click **Add inquiry** |
| **Supplier list or profile** | Click **Send inquiry** on one supplier, or select several and click **Send inquiry** in the action bar |
| **Product list or detail** | Click **Send inquiry** on a product |
| **AI agent** | Click **Send inquiry** on supplier search results |

To add suppliers to an inquiry that's already running, click the arrow next to **Send inquiry** and choose **Send to existing inquiry**.

## Choose suppliers

If you start from **Add inquiry**, the **Select supplier** window opens. Describe the suppliers you want and let the AI find them, click **Manual filter** to set [filters](/search-and-filters), or pick from one of your saved lists.

<Frame caption="Pick saved suppliers or describe new ones.">
  ![Select supplier window asking What kind of supplier do you want? with supplier list tabs across the top](/images/select-supplier.png)
</Frame>

Click **Select product** to choose the products to quote, or **Confirm** to go straight to the email without products. You can add products later.

<Note>
  Adding a supplier to an inquiry unlocks its contact details if they aren't unlocked yet. This costs 20 credits per supplier.
</Note>

### Selected supplier

Suppliers are grouped as **New** or **Existing**. Each group gets its own email template, so a first-time supplier can get an introduction while a long-time partner gets a shorter note. Change a supplier's group with the dropdown under its name.

Click **Edit contacts** to choose which email addresses receive the inquiry, or to add a contact name, email, or phone number.

### Selected product

Click **Add product** to choose products from your [product list](/product-list). Then click **Price & Qty** to set a **Target price (USD)** and **Quantity (pcs)** for each product. For products with variants, turn on **Fill by Variant** to set a price and quantity for each color, size, or other option.

### Basic info

Six fields that describe the inquiry: **Inquiry name**, **Order due date**, **Owner**, **Shipping address**, **Notes**, and **Attachments**. The first three are recommended. A yellow warning shows if they're missing.

### Preset questions

The questions every supplier should answer, such as unit price, production lead time, sample time, and offer validity. AI reads each reply and fills in the answers for you to [compare](/quotes-and-comparison).

To reuse questions, choose **Import from question template**, or **Import from files** to have AI pull questions out of a document. For each question you can set:

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Required** | Marks the question as a must-answer. [Follow-ups and auto replies](/follow-up-rules) can keep going until every required question is answered |
| **Add to initial questions** | Includes the question in the first email. The email template must contain the **Initial questions** variable |
| **Product-specific question** | Suppliers answer it separately for each product in an attached Excel file. Requires **Set questions as attachments** |
| **Label** | Ask the question to **all**, **new**, or **existing** suppliers only |

Turn on **Set questions as attachments** to send the questions as an Excel file attached to every email, instead of in the email body.

### Advance settings

**Auto cc** addresses, **AI follow up**, **AI auto reply**, and the inquiry's **knowledge base**. Toggle AI follow up and AI auto reply right from the panel, or click **Rules & bases** to configure them. See [Follow-up rules](/follow-up-rules).

## Write the email

The email editor shows one tab per supplier group, for example **Template for new suppliers**. Each tab starts from your default [email template](/email-templates).

- Grey chips like **Supplier name** or **Order due date** are variables. They're filled in for each supplier when the email is sent. Click `{}` to insert more.
- **Improve with AI** rewrites the email based on your instructions, for example _"Make it shorter and mention we're open to a trial order."_
- **Import template** replaces the email with a saved template. **Save as template** saves your edits for next time.
- Click the paperclip icon to attach files.
- Click **Preview** to see the email exactly as a specific supplier will receive it.

## Send

Click **Send now** to send the emails, or **Save as draft** to finish later. Once sent, the inquiry opens with its [overview](/quotes-and-comparison) so you can track replies.
