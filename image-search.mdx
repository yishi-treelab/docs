Have a photo but no product name? Image search finds visually similar products from a reference image, whether it's a competitor's product, a sample, a screenshot, or a sketch. It also shows you who makes and sells them.

## Search by image

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Image search">
    Click **Skills → Image search**. You can also drag, drop, or paste an image into the chat input and ask for similar products.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your image and a short note">
    Describe what matters most so the agent knows which features to prioritize.

    ```text
    Find similar outdoor shoes. The rotary (BOA-style) lacing is the key feature.
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the matches">
    The agent describes the key features it sees in your image, then lists the closest matches with supplier, MOQ, and rating.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="The agent reads your reference image first, then explains why each result matches.">

## Example prompts

- `Find similar acoustic pendant lights from this image`
- `Best matching cat puzzle toy boxes with pricing and MOQ`
- `Find this bag in vegan leather from suppliers with MOQ under 300`
- `Find similar products, then show me the top-rated suppliers that make them`
