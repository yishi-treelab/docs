Product research answers the questions you'd ask before choosing what to make or source. What's selling? Who's winning? What do buyers complain about? Where is demand heading? The agent pulls evidence from marketplaces, social platforms, and the web, then combines it into a single answer.

<Frame caption="A product research chat. Each card is a separate piece of research you can open.">
  ![Tiktok Trend Research](/images/tiktok-trend-research.png "Tiktok Trend Research")
</Frame>

## Data sources

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Amazon" icon="amazon">
    Best sellers, sales and price history, reviews, keywords, competitors, and category size.
  </Card>

  <Card title="TikTok Shop" icon="tiktok">
    Best-seller rankings, daily sales trends, and the creator videos driving sales.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Google Shopping & retailers" icon="google">
    Product listings and pricing across online retailers and brand sites.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Social & web" icon="globe">
    Reddit threads, YouTube videos, Google Trends, and news for demand signals and buyer sentiment.
  </Card>
</Columns>

When you select **Product research**, you can choose which marketplaces (TikTok, Amazon, Google Shopping) and which country to focus on. All three marketplaces are on by default.

## What you can ask

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Trends & demand">
    ```text
    What beauty and skincare products are trending among Gen Z in the US?
    ```

    ```text
    What are the fastest-growing keywords for phone cases?
    ```

    ```text
    Is demand for plant-based protein in Europe growing or slowing?
    ```
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Best sellers & competitors">
    ```text
    Top 20 best-selling golf polo shirts on Amazon US, with price and monthly sales
    ```

    ```text
    Which kitchen gadgets are going viral on TikTok Shop right now?
    ```

    ```text
    What are Lululemon's best sellers and how are they priced?
    ```
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Buyer feedback">
    ```text
    What do 1–3 star reviews say about the top 10 cat puzzle feeders on Amazon?
    ```

    ```text
    What do Reddit users complain about most with standing desks?
    ```
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Market sizing">
    ```text
    How big is the Amazon US market for reusable kitchen storage, and how concentrated is it by brand?
    ```
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## What you get back

Depending on the question, the agent returns one or more of:

- **Product grids**: listings with image, price, rating, and seller, which you can filter and sort
- **Charts**: sales, price, and search-interest trends over time
- **Comparison tables**: products or brands side by side

## From research to sourcing

Research usually leads straight into sourcing. Keep the same chat going:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find what's selling">
    ```text
    Identify easy-to-launch fashion products that are trending on TikTok Shop US
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Visualize your version">
    ```text
    Generate a product concept for #2 in sage green with a minimalist logo
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find who can make it">
    ```text
    Find low-MOQ suppliers that can make this
    ```
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Marketplace figures such as estimated sales come from third-party data providers. Treat them as directional estimates rather than exact numbers.
</Note>
