The knowledge base holds the facts the AI needs to talk to suppliers on your behalf. It's used whenever the AI writes inquiry emails, generates questions, sends [follow-ups](/follow-up-rules), or [auto-replies](/follow-up-rules#ai-auto-reply) to suppliers.

You don't need to add everything. **Your company info and the inquiry's details are included by default**: your company name, description, website, and customer base, plus the inquiry's products, quantities, target prices, due date, and shipping address.

## What to add

Add what suppliers ask about that isn't already in your company profile or the inquiry:

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Commercial terms" icon="handshake">
    Payment terms, Incoterms, currency, and whether you accept trial orders.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Quality & compliance" icon="certificate">
    Required certifications, testing standards, and audit requirements.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Packaging & labeling" icon="box">
    Carton specs, labeling rules, and barcode requirements.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Logistics" icon="truck">
    Preferred ports, forwarders, and shipping methods.
  </Card>
</Columns>

Each entry has **Base content** (text) and an optional **Attachment**, such as a supplier manual, a packaging spec sheet, or a quality standard PDF.

## Workspace knowledge

Workspace knowledge is shared by every inquiry, so add the things that are always true for your company here.

<Frame caption="Workspace knowledge bases in Settings → Inquiry.">
  ![Knowledge Bases](/images/knowledge-bases.png "Knowledge Bases")
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Knowledge bases">
    Go to **Settings → Inquiry → Knowledge bases**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an entry">
    Click **Add new**. Enter the **Base content**, and upload an **Attachment** if you have one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click anywhere outside the entry to save it. Click an entry to edit it, or use the trash icon to delete it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Inquiry knowledge

Some facts only apply to one RFQ, like a special packaging request for a single retailer or a project-specific test requirement. Add those to the inquiry itself:

1. Open **Advance settings**. Click **Rules & bases** while sending, or use the **Advance settings** card on the inquiry's overview.
2. Under **Knowledge bases**, click **Add new**.
3. Enter the content, attach any files, and click **Save**.

## Write good entries

<Tip>
  Write entries as plain, specific statements, the way you'd explain them to a supplier. Vague notes give the AI little to work with.
</Tip>

| Instead of | Write |
| --- | --- |
| _Standard terms_ | _We pay 30% deposit and 70% against the bill of lading copy. We don't accept letters of credit._ |
| _Good quality_ | _All products must pass AQL 2.5 inspection. We use a third-party inspector before shipment._ |
| _Ship fast_ | _We ship FOB Ningbo or FOB Shanghai. Our forwarder books the space._ |
