The **Orders** page is where you check on production. Each row is one order, with its health, a bar of its milestones, and the issue that needs attention. Open it from **Order → Orders** in the left sidebar.

</Frame>

## Find orders

- **Search orders** finds orders by PO #, style, and other text.
- **Filter** narrows the list by field. Click **Add condition** to combine several, then **Apply**.

<Frame caption="The Filter panel.">
  ![Orders Filter](/images/orders-filter.png "Orders Filter")
</Frame>

You can filter on **PO #**, **Style**, **Status**, **Health**, **Milestone profile**, **Supplier**, **WIP tracker**, **Owner**, **Risk**, **Customer**, **Program**, and the order's dates. The operators depend on the field:

| Field type | Operators |
| --- | --- |
| Text, such as PO # or Customer | **contains**, **equals**, **not equals**, **is empty**, **is not empty** |
| Choice, such as Status or Health | **equals**, **is any of**, **is none of**, **is empty**, **is not empty** |
| Linked records, such as Supplier or WIP tracker | **is any of**, **is none of**, **is empty**, **is not empty** |
| Dates, such as Ex-factory | **on or after**, **on or before**, **is empty**, **is not empty** |

<Tip>
  To find orders that still need a WIP tracker, filter **WIP tracker** **is empty**.
</Tip>

## Save a view

A view saves your filters, columns, and display settings as a tab. **All Orders** is always there.

1. Set up the filters and layout you want.
2. Click **\+ Add view**, give it a name, and click **Save**.
3. When you change a saved view, a **Save** button appears next to its tab. Click it to keep the changes.

Click **···** on an open view to **Rename** or **Delete** it.

### Add to a WIP tracker

<Frame caption="Choosing a tracker for the selected orders.">
  ![Add to WIP tracker dialog with a search box, a tracker named WIP Demo for Dongyang Lejing Garment Co., Ltd. with 3 POs, and a New WIP tracker with these POs link](/images/wip/orders-add-to-tracker.png)
</Frame>

Pick a tracker and click **Add**. A tracker is for one supplier, and an order can be in only one active tracker. So the dialog tells you how many orders are eligible, and skips any that are already in a tracker or belong to a different supplier.

### Apply a milestone profile

<Frame caption="Switching orders to a different milestone profile.">
  ![Apply milestone profile dialog with a profile picker and a warning that current steps are replaced and every reported plan date, actual date, and note is cleared](/images/wip/orders-apply-profile.png)
</Frame>

<Warning>
  Applying a profile to orders that already have one replaces their steps. Every reported plan date, actual date, and note on those orders is cleared, and this can't be undone.
</Warning>
